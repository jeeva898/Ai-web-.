GOD OF WAR TRILOGY

The God of War Greek-era trilogy consists of the original God of War (2005), God of War II (2007), and God of War III (2010). These hack-and-slash action-adventure games follow the Spartan warrior Kratos on a merciless quest for vengeance against the Olympian gods.

TRILER

The Original Trilogy

    God of War (2005): Introduces Kratos as a tormented servant of the gods who is tasked with defeating Ares, the original God of War, to free himself from his horrific visions.
    God of War II (2007): After being betrayed and stripped of his godhood by Zeus, Kratos travels to the Island of Creation to alter his fate and exact his revenge.
    God of War III (2010): The climactic finale where Kratos scales Mount Olympus and lays waste to the entire pantheon of Greek gods

Remake Trilogy

A full, ground-up remake collection of the original trilogy is in development for the PlayStation 5. Announced during the 20th-anniversary celebration, the project will feature updated visuals, and Kratos's original voice actor, TC Carson, will return to portray the character

releaseing platforms

Because the project was announced as a full remake rather than a simple remaster, it is being rebuilt from the ground up to utilize modern hardware. At launch, it is expected to be exclusive to the PlayStation 5, though—following PlayStation's recent release strategies—a PC port could potentially follow in later years.
